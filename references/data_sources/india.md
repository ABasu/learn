# India data sources

## Company reports and exchange filings
- NSE India — company announcements, results, shareholding, corporate actions
- BSE India — cross-check filings and corporate actions
- Company investor-relations website — annual reports, presentations, transcripts

## Market data
- NSE and BSE official sources
- Licensed market-data provider

## Macroeconomic data
- Reserve Bank of India
- Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation

## Financial history and peer comparison
- Screener.in — **mandatory for every Indian-market analysis**. Use the
  company consolidated page as the standard starting point, for example
  `https://www.screener.in/company/BOSCHLTD/consolidated/`. Cite it for
  financial history, ratios, peer comparisons and ownership summaries, and
  cross-check material figures against NSE/BSE filings or company disclosures.
