# India market overlay

Use INR, Ind AS, SEBI disclosures, Indian market hours, and Indian settlement and regulatory conventions. Examine promoter holdings, pledges, related-party transactions, and shareholding patterns.

## Required research source

Use Screener.in in every Indian-stock analysis. Start from the consolidated
company URL (`https://www.screener.in/company/<TICKER>/consolidated/`), cite it
in the Sources section, and use it for multi-year financial history, ratios,
peer comparisons and ownership context. Cross-check material current figures
against official NSE/BSE filings and company investor-relations material.

## Numeric convention

Use Indian financial notation throughout the report: rupees, lakhs (₹1 lakh = ₹100,000), and crores (₹1 crore = ₹10,000,000). For large values, write the full crore amount where practical, such as ₹12,450 crore; do not replace it with millions or billions. Preserve the source unit only when quoting a table, and convert it immediately in the narrative when needed.
